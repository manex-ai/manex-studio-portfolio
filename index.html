05 / Frequently asked

Good to know.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No upfront payment for the proposed standard package. Payment is due after you approve the completed agreed preview, before domain purchase and launch.

Are the portfolio businesses existing clients?

No. The examples are independent development concepts, not endorsements or evidence of paid client work.

Can you guarantee more customers?

No. A website supports discoverability, credibility and customer enquiries, but sales depend on many factors beyond the website.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. Contact MANEX with your requested updates and receive a separate quote based on the work required.

What happens after the included hosting period?

Renewal options and costs will be confirmed before the contract begins, with any future provider changes communicated before renewal.

Do you build online shops with payments and live inventory?

The $100 package is for a regular business website and enquiry-based features. Live stock systems, customer accounts, payment processing and other complex services are not included.
